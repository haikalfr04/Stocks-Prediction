Next-day return prediction for IDX stocks

Can a model predict tomorrow's return for Astra International, Bank Rakyat Indonesia and Telkom Indonesia well enough to beat simply holding the stock? Every number on this page is computed out-of-sample with walk-forward validation, compared against naive baselines, and backtested with real IDX transaction costs.

Method

  1. Data: daily adjusted prices from Yahoo Finance since 2015.
  2. Target: the next-day log return, not the price. Predicting the price level looks impressive on a chart but only copies yesterday's price.
  3. Features: lagged returns, rolling mean and volatility, distance from moving averages, RSI, MACD, intraday range and volume z-score. Each feature uses only data available at that day's close.
  4. Validation: walk-forward with an expanding window. Each model trains on at least 3 years of data, predicts the next month, then retrains. Test data is never used for training.
  5. Baselines: random walk (0%), historical mean and momentum. A model is only useful if it beats these.
  6. Backtest: long or cash only, since short selling is not generally available on IDX. Fees are included.

Limitations

  • Daily stock returns are close to unpredictable. Small edges in accuracy often disappear after fees.
  • The backtest assumes trades fill at the close price with no slippage.
  • Three stocks and one decade of data make for a small sample. Results can vary across regimes.
